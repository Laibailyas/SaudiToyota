Step 01 / 04

Terms and Conditions

Please read the terms below carefully before you register.

  1. 01

    Registration is not available for individuals under 18 years of age.

  2. 02

    Mobile phone photography is prohibited in the photographers’ area surrounding the track; only professional cameras are allowed.

  3. 03

    I commit to fully comply with the instructions of the race organizers both inside and outside the track.

  4. 04

    I acknowledge that I must submit all photographed content from the Saudi Toyota Championship to the Saudi Automobile & Motorcycle Federation via email at: Digitalmedia@samf.gov.sa

  5. 05

    I acknowledge and pledge not to use drones (unmanned aerial vehicles) without obtaining official permission from the General Authority of Civil Aviation. If permission is granted, I undertake to provide a copy of the permit to the Saudi Automobile & Motorcycle Federation.

  6. 06

    I fully understand that the Federation is not responsible for any legal or security consequences resulting from the unauthorized use of drones.

  7. 07

    I acknowledge that I am fully aware of the registration times, and the process for collecting the entry permit and safety vest.

  8. 08

    I understand that no registration requests will be accepted after the deadline announced on the official Saudi Toyota Championship website and its social media channels.

  9. 09

    I acknowledge that I must collect the entry permit and safety vest on the first day of the championship.
